Offshore geotechnics

Support vector regression for carbonate gravel and Monte Carlo analysis of caisson installation. Undergraduate research at Tianjin University, 2021–22.

Undergraduate research, Civil Engineering, Tianjin University (July 2021 – March 2022). Supervisor: Prof. Sa Li.

Schematic of a tubular caisson penetrating a seabed whose soil strength varies in space.
  • Implemented support vector regression with particle swarm optimisation in MATLAB to model the mechanical behaviour of carbonate gravel (Liu et al., 2023).
  • Contributed Monte Carlo simulations of the maximum inclination angle of a large tubular caisson installed in a spatially varying seabed (Zhang et al., 2022).
